Home Renovation Contractor in Toronto

Kitchens, bathrooms, basements and additions across Ontario — turning houses into homes since 2009.

Turning ugly homes into masterpiece living spaces

That's how Daramax Services started in 2009 — with a love of construction design and a mission to turn tired houses into homes people are proud of. More than 15 years and 500+ projects later, we remain a residential renovation specialist serving homeowners across Ontario.

You work directly with our in-house team of skilled tradespeople and project managers — no subcontractors — for every phase of your project. We treat your home with respect: punctual crews, clear communication, and a clean, organized work environment from demolition to final touches.

What we build

  • Kitchen renovations — layouts, cabinetry, countertops, lighting and full gut renovations
  • Bathroom renovations — from powder rooms to spa-style ensuites
  • Basement renovations — rec rooms, home offices, guest suites and legal second units
  • Full home remodeling — whole-house transformations, inside and out
  • Additions — extra bedrooms, extensions and expanded living space
  • Interior and exterior services — flooring, stairs, bars, living spaces, landscaping, driveways and facades

Fixed prices, no surprises

Renovation horror stories almost always come down to two things: price and communication. We solve both:

  • Transparent, upfront pricing — fixed prices agreed before work begins, no hidden fees
  • On-time delivery — we guarantee timely completion, respecting your schedule and budget
  • Quality workmanship guaranteed — every project backed by our commitment to superior craftsmanship
  • Drawings and permits handled — architectural, interior, structural and MEP drawings plus end-to-end permit services
  • Multilingual team — clear communication in the language you're comfortable with
