Renovation Services for Property Managers Across Ontario

Unit turnovers, common areas and multi-phase building upgrades — one reliable renovation partner for your whole portfolio.

One contractor for the whole portfolio

Property managers juggle turnovers, tenant complaints, aging common areas and capital projects — usually with a different contractor for each. Daramax Services has served Ontario since 2009 as a single, dependable renovation partner for residential and commercial portfolios of every size.

Our commercial projects include both new construction and repairs and restorations. Occupied and fully operational job sites are never a problem: we plan, manage and build multi-phase jobs so tenants can stay put while we upgrade the building around them.

Because our team is in-house — skilled tradespeople, project managers and support staff, no subcontractors — you get consistent quality, one point of contact, and crews that already know your buildings and your standards.

What we handle

  • Unit turnovers — fast, clean make-readies between tenants: paint, flooring, kitchens, bathrooms and repairs
  • Common areas — lobbies, hallways, laundry rooms, amenity spaces and building entrances
  • Exterior and envelope — facades, entrances, parking areas and curb-appeal upgrades
  • Repairs and restorations — from water-damage repair to full restoration of tired units
  • Capital projects — multi-unit and multi-phase upgrades planned around occupancy

Built for how you work

  • Fast turnovers — we understand vacancy costs money; schedules are built for speed without cutting corners
  • Tenant-friendly crews — punctual, respectful, clean job sites; our multilingual team communicates clearly with residents
  • Transparent pricing — fixed upfront prices, no hidden fees — easy to approve and easy to budget
  • One call for everything — a single partner for turnovers, common areas and capital work across your portfolio
  • Documentation you need — drawings and permit services handled end-to-end when the job requires them

On time, on budget, every time

We guarantee timely project completion, respecting your schedule and budget. Our proven track record of completing projects on time and within budget is why property managers keep calling us back building after building.

Whether it's a single unit turnover or a multi-phase capital upgrade, every project is backed by our commitment to superior quality and craftsmanship.
