Commercial Renovation Contractor in Toronto

Office, retail and industrial renovations across Ontario — planned, managed and built by one accountable in-house team since 2009.

Renovations that keep your business running

A commercial renovation is not like a home project. There are tenants, customers, employees and revenue on the line. Daramax Services has been renovating commercial spaces in Ontario since 2009, and our specialty is the hard part: occupied, fully operational job sites.

Our commercial projects include both new construction and repairs and restorations. We plan, manage and build multi-phase jobs, sequencing the work so your business stays open and your people stay safe while we transform the space around them.

Because we work with our own in-house team of skilled tradespeople, project managers and support staff — no subcontractors — you deal with one accountable crew from the first site walk to the final walkthrough. That means clearer communication, tighter schedules and fixed, transparent pricing with no surprises.

What we renovate

  • Offices — full office fit-outs, reconfigurations, meeting rooms, reception areas and washrooms
  • Retail spaces — storefronts, showrooms, restaurants and customer-facing interiors
  • Industrial and warehouse — offices within industrial buildings, staff areas and facility upgrades
  • Common and amenity areas — lobbies, corridors, elevators lobbies and building entrances
  • Repairs and restorations — bringing tired or damaged commercial space back to professional standard

Our occupied-site approach

Most contractors want an empty building. We built our reputation on occupied ones. Our multi-phase approach breaks your project into sequenced stages so that:

  • Your business stays open — work is phased around your operating hours, including evenings and weekends when needed
  • Tenants and staff stay safe — dust control, barriers, clear signage and daily clean-up are part of every plan
  • Communication stays constant — one project manager, regular progress updates, and a schedule you can plan around
  • Quality never slips — the same in-house crew and the same quality standards on every phase

From drawings to permits to close-out

Commercial work lives and dies on paperwork. We provide comprehensive construction drawings — architectural, interior design, structural, and MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) — and end-to-end permit services, so your renovation clears the City's requirements without stalling your timeline.

Our track record: projects completed on time and within budget, with fixed upfront pricing and a commitment to superior quality and craftsmanship on every job.
