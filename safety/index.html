Our Safety Commitment

At Daramax Services, safety is not just a policy—it's a core value that guides every decision we make. We believe that a strong safety culture is the foundation of quality work and successful projects.

Comprehensive Safety Policies

Detailed safety protocols and procedures for all construction activities, ensuring compliance with industry standards and regulations.

Regular Training Programs

Ongoing safety training for all team members, including first aid, equipment operation, and emergency response procedures.

Personal Protective Equipment

High-quality PPE provided to all workers, including helmets, safety glasses, gloves, and appropriate footwear.

Safety Goals & Metrics

Clear safety objectives with regular monitoring and reporting to keep every jobsite safe.

Building a Safety Culture

Our safety culture is built on the principle that every team member has the right to go home safely at the end of each day. We foster an environment where safety is everyone's responsibility.

  • Daily safety briefings before work begins
  • Regular safety inspections and audits
  • Immediate reporting and investigation of near-misses
  • Continuous improvement of safety procedures
  • Employee involvement in safety planning
  • Regular equipment maintenance and inspection

Safety Excellence

Safety is built into everything we do — from daily briefings to regular inspections, we work to keep every jobsite safe for our crew and your property.

Certifications & Training

Our team maintains the highest standards of safety training and certification to ensure we meet and exceed industry requirements.

  • First Aid & CPR Certified Team Members
  • Working at Heights Training
  • Confined Space Entry Training
  • WHMIS Training for All Staff
