Our History

Founded in 2009 by the owner who has been in the industry since 2003, and for the love of construction design, he started operating Daramax Services while still working with his masters to hunt his own lifelong dreams—turning ugly homes into masterpiece living spaces.

Since 2009, Daramax Services has been providing quality construction services for over 17 years. Our team has completed a wide variety of projects including commercial buildings, residential homes, and industrial facilities. We pride ourselves on speed, attention to detail, reliability, and commitment to customer satisfaction—guaranteed end results on every project completed by the Daramax Services Team, strongly valuable from start to completion.

We take every step to ensure each new project is treated like our first, continuing our company's mission: a future reputation as a solid business foundation for generations ahead to count on. Therefore, our first customer satisfaction is treated like the last customer project started.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Ontario. We are an Ontario-based company operating province-wide — including the Greater Toronto Area — for residential, commercial, hotel and property-management projects. Wherever you are in Ontario, just call 416-477-8561 and ask.

How do I get a quote?

Call us at 416-477-8561 or fill out the Get Free Quote form below. Tell us about your project and we'll arrange a free consultation to discuss scope, timeline and pricing.

What size projects do you take on?

We handle everything from single-room renovations like kitchens and bathrooms to full home remodels, multi-phase commercial renovations, and occupied hotel and property-management work. If it's a real construction project, we're the right size for it.

Do you work on occupied buildings?

Yes. Occupied and fully operational job sites are never a problem for us. We plan and manage multi-phase work so offices, hotels and rental properties can keep running while we renovate — with clear communication and minimal disruption.

Do you handle permits and drawings?

Yes. We provide comprehensive construction drawings — architectural, interior design, structural, and MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) — plus end-to-end permit services for all general construction needs.

How do your prices work?

We work on fixed prices with no surprises: transparent, upfront pricing with no hidden fees or unexpected costs. You'll know the price before work begins, and we stick to it.
