Hotel & Motel Renovation Services in Toronto

Guest rooms, lobbies and full-property upgrades across Ontario — phased so your property keeps welcoming guests while we work.

Renovation without closing your doors

Every empty room is lost revenue. Daramax Services renovates hotels and motels across Ontario with one rule: your property stays open. Since 2009 we have planned, managed and built phased hospitality projects where guests keep checking in while we transform the building around them.

Our commercial projects include both new construction and repairs and restorations. Occupied and fully operational job sites are never a problem — we sequence the work floor by floor and wing by wing, with dust control, noise management and clear guest communication built into every phase.

You work with our own in-house team of skilled tradespeople and project managers — no subcontractors — so the crew that starts your renovation is the crew that finishes it, with the same quality standards in room one and room one hundred.

What we renovate

  • Guest rooms and suites — full room refreshes: bathrooms, flooring, lighting, millwork and finishes
  • Lobbies and reception areas — first-impression spaces that set the tone for the whole property
  • Corridors and common areas — hallways, elevators lobbies, stairwells and amenity spaces
  • Exteriors and entrances — facades, canopies, parking areas and curb appeal
  • Back-of-house — staff areas, laundry, storage and service corridors

Phased work, zero drama

Hospitality renovation is logistics as much as construction. Our multi-phase approach means:

  • Rooms stay sellable — we renovate in blocks, so the rest of your inventory keeps earning
  • Guests barely notice — quiet hours respected, work zones sealed and signed, daily clean-up
  • Brand standards met — finishes and details executed to match your flag or boutique identity
  • One point of contact — a dedicated project manager coordinating every trade and every phase

Drawings, permits and fixed pricing

We provide comprehensive construction drawings — architectural, interior design, structural, and MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) — and end-to-end permit services, so approvals don't stall your schedule.

And like every Daramax project, you get fixed, transparent pricing with no hidden fees: the price we agree is the price you pay.
